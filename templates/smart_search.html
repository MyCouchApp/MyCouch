Using Smart Search with Discord

Prefer searching from the couch? MyCouch can bring Smart Search directly into Discord.

Once Discord integration has been configured by your MyCouch administrator, use the /search command in Discord followed by what you're looking for.

Smart Search understands natural language, so you don't need to know the exact title. For example:

  • /search funny movie from the 90s
  • /search sci-fi movies
  • /search something with Tom Hanks
  • /search short movie for tonight

MyCouch searches your Plex library and returns matching titles directly in Discord, making it easy to find something to watch without opening MyCouch.

No Plex password or MyCouch login is required to use /search in Discord. Access is controlled through your Discord server.
