What is MyCouch?

MyCouch is a companion for your Plex server that makes it easier to explore your library, discover something to watch, and see what's happening on your server.

Smart Search lets you search your Plex library using natural language. Ask for something like “a funny 90s movie” or “a short thriller for tonight” and MyCouch will find matching titles.

My History shows your personal Plex viewing history, making it easy to see what you've watched.

Movies & TV let you browse and explore the content available on your Plex server.

Server Stats gives you an overview of your Plex library and helps you understand what's taking up space.

Discord Integration brings Smart Search to your Discord server using the /search command, so you can find something to watch without opening MyCouch.

MyCouch runs alongside Plex and is designed to give you a simple, friendly way to get more from your media library.
